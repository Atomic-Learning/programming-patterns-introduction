# What Is a Programming Pattern?

A programming pattern is a reusable structure for solving a common type of problem. Over time, programmers have developed many such patterns to solve recurring tasks and many programmers will be aware of and apply them.
Patterns are not fixed programs. They are templates you adapt to different data and goals. They can often be implemented in multiple programming languages.

# Why Patterns Help

* They reduce the need for reinventing the wheel by allowing reuse of proven structures.
* They make code easier to read because the structure is familiar to the reader.
* They support problem-solving by higher-level steps that can be combined to complte a task.
